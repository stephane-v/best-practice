# Customer Acquisition Strategies - Best Practices

Customer acquisition is the process of attracting and gaining new customers for your business. A successful acquisition strategy combines multiple channels and tactics to reach your target audience effectively while maintaining a sustainable cost per acquisition (CPA).

## 1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

### Overview
SEO is the practice of optimizing your online presence to increase organic (non-paid) visibility in search engine results pages (SERPs).

### Key Components

#### Technical SEO
- **Site Speed**: Optimize loading times (target < 3 seconds)
- **Mobile Optimization**: Ensure responsive design
- **Site Architecture**: Clear URL structure and internal linking
- **Schema Markup**: Implement structured data
- **SSL Certificate**: Use HTTPS across all pages

#### On-Page SEO
- **Keyword Research**: Identify high-value, relevant keywords
- **Title Tags**: Include primary keyword, keep under 60 characters
- **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling descriptions under 160 characters
- **Header Structure**: Use H1-H6 tags logically
- **Image Optimization**: Alt tags, compression, descriptive filenames

#### Off-Page SEO
- **Link Building**: Earn quality backlinks from authoritative sites
- **Guest Posting**: Contribute valuable content to industry blogs
- **Digital PR**: Create newsworthy content for media coverage
- **Local SEO**: Optimize Google Business Profile

### SEO Checklist

```markdown
□ Conduct keyword research for target pages
□ Optimize title tags and meta descriptions
□ Ensure mobile-friendly design
□ Improve page load speed
□ Build internal linking structure
□ Create a sitemap.xml
□ Set up Google Search Console
□ Implement schema markup
□ Develop a link building strategy
□ Monitor rankings and traffic
```

### Tools Recommended
- Google Search Console (free)
- Ahrefs or SEMrush (keyword research)
- Screaming Frog (technical audits)
- Google PageSpeed Insights (performance)

## 2. Content Marketing

### Overview
Content marketing focuses on creating and distributing valuable, relevant content to attract and engage a defined target audience.

### Content Types by Funnel Stage

| Stage | Content Type | Goal |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Awareness | Blog posts, Videos, Infographics | Educate and attract |
| Interest | Ebooks, Webinars, Case Studies | Demonstrate expertise |
| Consideration | Product comparisons, Demos, Free trials | Show value |
| Decision | Testimonials, Reviews, Consultations | Build trust |

### Content Strategy Framework

#### 1. Define Your Audience
```
Buyer Persona Template:
├── Demographics (age, location, job title)
├── Goals and challenges
├── Information sources
├── Objections and concerns
└── Decision-making process
```

#### 2. Content Pillars
Create 3-5 main topics that align with:
- Your expertise
- Customer pain points
- Business objectives
- Search demand

#### 3. Content Calendar
```
Weekly Content Schedule Example:
├── Monday: Blog post (educational)
├── Wednesday: Social media content
├── Thursday: Newsletter
└── Friday: Video or podcast content
```

### Content Best Practices

1. **Quality over Quantity**: Focus on depth and value
2. **Consistency**: Maintain a regular publishing schedule
3. **Repurpose**: Transform one piece into multiple formats
4. **Optimize**: Always include SEO elements
5. **Promote**: Distribute across multiple channels
6. **Measure**: Track engagement and conversion

### Content Promotion Checklist
```markdown
□ Share on social media platforms
□ Send to email subscribers
□ Submit to relevant communities
□ Reach out for backlinks
□ Repurpose into different formats
□ Run paid promotion (if budget allows)
□ Update and refresh evergreen content
```

## 3. Paid Advertising

### Overview
Paid advertising allows you to reach your target audience quickly through various paid channels and formats.

### Pay-Per-Click (PPC) Advertising

#### Google Ads Structure
```
Account
└── Campaigns (Budget, targeting)
    └── Ad Groups (Keywords, ads)
        └── Ads (Headlines, descriptions)
            └── Keywords (Match types)
```

#### Keyword Match Types
| Match Type | Symbol | Example | Matches |
|------------|--------|---------|---------|
| Broad Match | none | running shoes | related searches |
| Phrase Match | "keyword" | "running shoes" | includes phrase |
| Exact Match | [keyword] | [running shoes] | exact or close variant |

#### PPC Best Practices
1. **Start with exact/phrase match**: Control spend early
2. **Use negative keywords**: Exclude irrelevant searches
3. **Write compelling ad copy**: Include benefits and CTAs
4. **Test ad variations**: A/B test headlines and descriptions
5. **Optimize landing pages**: Ensure message match
6. **Monitor Quality Score**: Improve relevance and CTR

### Social Media Advertising

#### Platform Selection Guide

| Platform | Best For | Audience |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Facebook/Meta | B2C, broad reach | All demographics |
| Instagram | Visual products, lifestyle | 18-44 years |
| LinkedIn | B2B, professional services | Professionals |
| TikTok | Gen Z, viral content | 16-30 years |
| Twitter/X | News, tech, thought leadership | News consumers |

#### Ad Creative Best Practices
- **Visual Impact**: Use high-quality images/videos
- **Clear Value Proposition**: State the benefit immediately
- **Strong CTA**: Tell users exactly what to do
- **Mobile-First**: Design for mobile viewing
- **Test Formats**: Carousel, video, static images

### Display Advertising
- **Remarketing**: Target previous website visitors
- **Contextual**: Show ads on relevant content
- **Programmatic**: Automated ad buying

### Budget Allocation Framework
```
Suggested Initial Split:
├── Search Ads (Google/Bing): 40%
├── Social Ads: 35%
├── Remarketing: 15%
└── Experimental: 10%
```

## 5. Email Marketing for Acquisition

### Overview
Email marketing remains one of the most effective channels for customer acquisition when combined with lead magnets and nurture sequences.

### Lead Magnet Ideas
- **Ebooks/Guides**: In-depth resources on relevant topics
- **Templates**: Ready-to-use tools and frameworks
- **Checklists**: Step-by-step action items
- **Webinars**: Live or recorded educational sessions
- **Free Tools**: Calculators, assessments, generators
- **Discounts**: First-time purchase offers

### Email Capture Best Practices
```
High-Converting Opt-in Elements:
├── Compelling headline
├── Clear value proposition
├── Simple form (name + email)
├── Strong CTA button
├── Social proof (subscriber count, testimonials)
└── Privacy assurance
```

### Welcome Sequence Framework
```
Day 0: Welcome + Deliver lead magnet
Day 1: Introduction to brand/story
Day 3: Valuable content piece
Day 5: Case study/social proof
Day 7: Soft offer or next step
```

## 6. Referral and Partnership Marketing

### Overview
Leverage existing customers and partners to acquire new customers at a lower cost.

### Referral Program Elements
1. **Clear Incentive**: Both referrer and referee should benefit
2. **Easy Process**: Simple sharing mechanism
3. **Tracking**: Reliable attribution system
4. **Communication**: Regular reminders and updates

### Referral Program Examples
| Type | Referrer Gets | Referee Gets |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| Discount | $20 credit | 20% off first order |
| Free Product | Free month | Free month |
| Cash | $50 cash | $25 cash |
| Points | 500 points | 500 points |

### Partnership Types
- **Affiliate Marketing**: Commission-based referrals
- **Co-Marketing**: Joint campaigns with complementary brands
- **Integration Partners**: Technology partnerships
- **Influencer Partnerships**: Long-term ambassador programs

## 8. Measuring Acquisition Success

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### Traffic Metrics
- Total visitors
- Traffic by source/channel
- New vs. returning visitors
- Bounce rate by source

#### Lead Metrics
- Total leads generated
- Leads by source
- Lead quality score
- Cost per lead (CPL)

#### Customer Metrics
- New customers acquired
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- CAC by channel
- Time to first purchase

### CAC Calculation
```
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) =
Total Sales & Marketing Spend / Number of New Customers

Example:
$10,000 marketing spend / 100 new customers = $100 CAC
```

### Benchmarks to Target
- **CAC:LTV Ratio**: Target 1:3 or better
- **Payback Period**: < 12 months for most businesses
- **Conversion Rate**: 2-5% for most industries

## Acquisition Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Spreading too thin**: Focus on 2-3 channels initially
2. **Ignoring unit economics**: Always track CAC and ROI
3. **Poor targeting**: Invest in audience research
4. **Weak landing pages**: Optimize for conversion
5. **No attribution**: Implement proper tracking
6. **Inconsistency**: Maintain efforts over time
7. **Ignoring mobile**: Optimize all experiences for mobile
