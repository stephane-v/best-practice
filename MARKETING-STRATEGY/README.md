## Key Metrics to Track

### Acquisition Metrics
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: Total marketing spend / Number of leads
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Total spend / Number of new customers
- **Traffic Sources**: Organic, Paid, Social, Referral, Direct
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Clicks / Impressions × 100

### Conversion Metrics
- **Conversion Rate**: Conversions / Total visitors × 100
- **Lead-to-Customer Rate**: Customers / Leads × 100
- **Landing Page Conversion Rate**: Conversions / Landing page visits × 100
- **Cart Abandonment Rate**: Abandoned carts / Total carts × 100

### Sales Metrics
- **Average Deal Size**: Total revenue / Number of deals
- **Sales Cycle Length**: Average time from first contact to close
- **Win Rate**: Won deals / Total deals × 100
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Average revenue per customer × Average customer lifespan

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Understand Your Audience
- Define buyer personas
- Map the customer journey
- Identify pain points and motivations

### Step 2: Build Your Acquisition Engine
- Choose appropriate channels
- Create compelling content
- Implement tracking and analytics

### Step 3: Optimize for Conversion
- Design high-converting landing pages
- Implement A/B testing
- Nurture leads with email sequences

### Step 4: Close Sales Effectively
- Qualify leads properly
- Follow a structured sales process
- Handle objections professionally
