🧠 Engineering Convictions

A few principles that guide the technical choices on client work.

Digital sovereignty in professional and sensitive contexts

When a client handles regulated or public sector data, that data stays inside infrastructure we control. Sovereignty is a requirement here, not a marketing word.

Self-hosted by default for GDPR or public clients

For projects under GDPR, or with public sector clients, self-hosted is the default choice. A managed third-party service is an exception that has to be justified.

LLM in the EU rather than US hyperscalers for client data

Client data goes to models hosted in the EU (Mistral, or Ollama on our own servers). US hyperscaler APIs are avoided for anything that carries client information.

Pragmatism for personal use

On personal projects I stay pragmatic. The rules above exist to protect client data, they are not a dogma applied to everything.
